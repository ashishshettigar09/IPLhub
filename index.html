THE VERDICT: IPL Mega Auction 2027 — Retention & RTM Rules

1. Starting Purse & Retention Limit

Every team begins with ₹125 Crore Starting Purse. Each team may retain a maximum of 5 players before the auction. A team may choose to retain fewer than 5 players.

3. RTM Conversion Rule

Any unused retention slot is automatically converted into one Right to Match (RTM) card. The maximum combined number of retentions and RTMs is therefore 5.

5. Uncapped & RTM Usage Rule

The ₹8 Cr and ₹4 Cr values apply only when the respective retention slot is occupied by an uncapped player. An RTM card can be used during the auction to reclaim an eligible former player by matching the final winning bid, deducted from remaining purse.
